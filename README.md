About Selvax

Selvax Crates is a project developed by the same developer behind Selvax Client.

Selvax originally started as a Minecraft client focused on improving the player's experience through performance, customization, cosmetics, and quality-of-life features. However, Selvax is no longer limited to being just a client.

With Selvax Crates, we are taking the first steps toward expanding the Selvax ecosystem beyond the client itself, exploring new areas of Minecraft development including Plugins, Mods, and other gameplay-focused projects.

The goal is simple: Selvax should be more than a single Minecraft client — it should become a collection of projects built to enhance different parts of the Minecraft experience.

Selvax Crates is part of this expansion, bringing the Selvax name into the world of server-side plugins while maintaining the same focus on quality, customization, and a polished player experience.
